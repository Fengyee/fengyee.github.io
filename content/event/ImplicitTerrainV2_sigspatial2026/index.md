ImplicitTerrainV2 is a wavelet-guided, spatially adaptive implicit neural representation for digital elevation models, a follow-up to [ImplicitTerrain](https://fengyee.github.io/implicit-terrain/) toward a compact, efficient neural terrain data format.

More in the [paper](../../publication/feng-2026-implicitterrain-v2/), the [project page](https://fengyee.github.io/implicitterrainv2/), and the [slides](/uploads/sigspatial-2026/implicitterrainv2-oral-slides.pdf).
